Record in browser

Pick a browser tab or full screen in the share dialog for system audio. In Chrome or Edge, also check Share tab audio or Share system audio in the picker — selecting a surface alone is not enough. Many standalone app windows do not expose audio to the browser. You can combine tab/screen capture with your microphone below.

With Chrome or Edge, starting a recording opens a floating window (Document Picture-in-Picture) so the timer and Stop / mute controls stay visible while you use other apps.
